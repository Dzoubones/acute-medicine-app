# Acute Medical Take Android privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Acute Medical Take is published by AMT DIGITAL HEALTH LIMITED. This policy covers the Android application with package name `org.acutemedicaltake.app`.

## App data

The Android app has no AMT account or sign-in requirement. AMT does not collect or upload your checklist entries, favourites, timer settings, learning progress or personal notes. These features use storage on your device. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Personal notes are encrypted using AES-GCM and a key held by Android Keystore. Opening or saving notes requires supported biometric or device-credential authentication. Android handles authentication. AMT does not receive your fingerprint, facial data, device passcode or biometric templates. The app disables Android cloud backup for its app data.

Do not enter patient names, hospital or NHS numbers, dates of birth, addresses or other patient-identifiable information into the app. The app is intended for education and professional reference.

## Purchases

AMT Pro Lifetime is an optional one-time purchase processed through Google Play. The app queries Google Play for product information and purchase ownership, acknowledges completed purchases and stores an entitlement flag locally. AMT does not receive payment-card details. Google processes purchase and account information under its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Permissions and notifications

Internet and network-state permissions support opening external guidance and checking connectivity. Optional notification permission enables reminders that you start on the device. Reminder notifications use a timer title and a general review message. You can disable notifications in Android settings.

## External websites

The complete Acute Medical Take website and external guidance open only after you select a link. They open in a browser view and are subject to the relevant website's privacy policy. This policy covers the bundled Android application. Website services may handle information separately from the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you clear or delete it, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. Take Mode and personal notes also provide deletion controls within the app. Clearing local data does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. Purchase records are retained by Google under its policies, and eligible purchases can be restored through Google Play.

If you email AMT for support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to handle your enquiry. Send a deletion enquiry to admin@acutemedicaltake.org.

## Contact and updates

AMT DIGITAL HEALTH LIMITED can be contacted at admin@acutemedicaltake.org. Material changes to the app's privacy practices will be reflected in an updated policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures.
