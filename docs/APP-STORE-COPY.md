## Description

Acute Medical Take is an educational clinical navigator for clinicians,
international medical graduates who are new to the UK system, newly qualified
doctors and healthcare professionals developing confidence on the acute
medical take.

The app organises common acute presentations by specialty and provides concise
red flags, initial assessment steps, clinical tools, downloadable learning
summaries and routes to authoritative professional guidance.

Clinical information is educational. It does not replace clinical judgement,
senior advice, direct patient assessment, current BNF or SmPC information,
local Trust policy or emergency protocols.
